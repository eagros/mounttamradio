The record

This album is dedicated to the memory of Dave Smith, who conceived and played all of the guitar parts on this album. Sadly Dave fell to West Nile and Covid, and the project was shelved. In 2023 I met James Henry and Richard Howell, who inspired me to complete the album and contributed nearly all of the remaining parts. Special thanks also to Ed Quamen, Andy Moorer and Dave Aguilar.
